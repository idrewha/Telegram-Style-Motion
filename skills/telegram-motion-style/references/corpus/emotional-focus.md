## Adaptation decisions

Decide what the accent communicates before choosing its artwork: discovery before action, acknowledgement after a real result, or living character/identity during a hold. These require different triggers. In569 the star burst introduces Add Style **before** selection; treating it as post-success confetti would change the story. Alien and Theater emoji keep animating during forms and example holds, providing identity continuity rather than a new major attention beat each cycle.

Build independently controllable tracks when reproducing this variant: target anchor, large outlined reveal, small particles, glow/glints and optional contact ring. Start from the source position in the same screen-to-camera transform as the interface. Permit intentional bezel spill with a foreground layer outside the display mask. Preserve a clean center for the semantic icon; keep less important particles around it. If the source is still moving, track it through the entrance, then define what happens to residual particles when the source leaves.

Separate contour growth and thinning from particle travel, rotation, scale and disappearance. A small glint can remain after the principal shape dissolves. End the dominant accent before unrelated fine selection or reading; a short residual tail may overlap the related transition when it does not obscure the next target. No zoom is required by this source. Choose timing for the new task's reading needs; its short contact cadence is not an approved universal default.

For a future asset library, retain provenance, native frame rate/duration, transparent edges, visible-content bounds, anchor, entrance/loop/exit ranges and whether the asset can finish gracefully. A looping emoji alone does not supply a suitable discovery entrance or particle exit. This study does not yet collect or authorize a Telegram sticker library, and no asset pack is currently bundled here.
