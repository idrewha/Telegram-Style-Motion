- **Эмоциональный фокус**: stars, emoji, particles and expressive accents, including target attachment and pre-action/transition-tail timing: [emotional focus](emotional-focus.md).
- Coherent content movement, deceleration and next-target anticipation: [scrolling](scroll.md).
- Continuous slider feedback, voice recording/preview/send, action-to-toast morph, countdown and celebration: [control feedback](control-feedback.md).
- Text replacement, sustained long press and origin-linked menu transformations: [input and surfaces](input-and-surfaces.md).
- AI waiting, progressive results, local corrections and reading clocks: [AI response rhythm](ai-response.md).
- Readable holds with secondary motion, success and loop endings: [pacing](pacing.md).
- Whole-interface and mockup scale, feature-target framing, coupled translation, bounce/settling and zoom quality: [camera composition](../camera-motion.md).
- Existing approved tap/camera dynamics remain in [approved motion](../approved-motion.md). Do not replace those defaults with one source's faster pace.

For frame landmarks of a specific technique, search the [checked event records](data/deep-events.json) by `entity`; this contains only the whole-frame-pass sources explicitly listed in the file and keeps their limits. It excludes inventory-only candidates. Read the relevant recipe first rather than loading all event records. For the complete communication sequence around an effect, consult the [scene records](data/deep-scenes.json): story, framing, holds, emotional interpretation and remaining limits.

## Portable archive boundary

The source inventory and review declarations are inherited from the local study,
not a new visual review performed during packaging. Original MP4s, dense contact
sheets and full review ledgers remain outside this distribution. Paths beginning
`external-study-archive/` identify omitted historical evidence, not bundled files
or runtime inputs. Included CSVs, measurement JSONs and selected proof images
retain the original uncertainties and rejected measurements. Public post links
identify the sources; no reference-video collection is redistributed.
